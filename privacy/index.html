Privacy notice

Effective 29 August 2026. Bike Service Timeline stores bikes, components, service entries, mileage, notes, and attachments in this browser.

The app does not send those records to another origin.

Exports and deletion

JSON and CSV exports are created in this browser and downloaded to your device. You control where those files go.

Delete records in the app or remove the site’s stored data in browser settings. Uninstalling may not clear browser storage.

Sample data

Demo records use a separate browser database. Reset demo clears it. Start for real deletes demo changes before opening your records.

Network use

The app loads scripts and requests app files only from this site.

After the first visit, the saved app and your records work offline.

Your responsibility

There is no server copy of your records. Download JSON backups before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
